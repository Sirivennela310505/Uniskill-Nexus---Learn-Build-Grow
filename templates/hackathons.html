💡 Quick Hackathon Tips for B.Tech Students

⭐ Register early — slots fill up fast
⭐ Team of 3-4 works best — divide frontend, backend, presentation
⭐ Focus on solving a real problem — judges love impact
⭐ Build an MVP in first 12 hours, polish later
⭐ Prepare a 3-minute demo — practice it multiple times
⭐ Participation itself builds resume and confidence
⭐ Use GitHub from day one — judges check your commits
⭐ Don't aim for perfection — aim for a working demo
