📋 How to Apply — Step by Step

  1. Build at least 1 skill (Python, Web, Java, etc.) to a beginner level
  2. Create a simple resume with your skills, education, and any projects
  3. Make a GitHub profile and upload at least 1-2 small projects
  4. Register on Internshala or LinkedIn with a complete profile
  5. Apply to 5-10 internships per day — don't wait for one reply
  6. Write a short cover letter explaining why you are interested
  7. Prepare for a basic interview — they may ask about your projects
  8. Follow up after 3-5 days if you don't hear back

💡 Tips to Get Selected

⭐ Start early — apply from 2nd year itself, don't wait for final year
⭐ Don't focus on stipend first — experience matters more as a fresher
⭐ Virtual internships are equally valid — don't ignore them
⭐ Keep your LinkedIn updated — many recruiters directly message students
⭐ One good project is better than 10 certificates with no proof of work
⭐ Be honest in interviews — say "I am learning" instead of faking knowledge
⭐ Apply even if you feel underqualified — you will learn during the internship
