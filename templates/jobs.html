💡 Job Application Tips for B.Tech Students

⭐ Apply to 10-15 jobs per day — volume matters for freshers
⭐ Customize your resume for each job using their keywords
⭐ Follow up after 5 days if no reply via LinkedIn or email
⭐ LinkedIn is important — keep profile 100% complete with photo
⭐ Practice DSA on LeetCode daily — most companies test this
⭐ Don't apply only to big companies — startups are great for learning
⭐ Off-campus drives on FreshersWorld often have less competition
⭐ Never lie on your resume — be honest about your skill level
